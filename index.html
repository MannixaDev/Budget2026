    Budget jargon, decoded

    OBR
    The Office for Budget Responsibility. It's the independent watchdog that publishes the official forecasts for growth, borrowing and inflation alongside the Budget.
    Fiscal headroom
    How much room the Chancellor has before breaking their own borrowing rules. Less headroom means less money for giveaways.
    Fiscal drag
    When tax thresholds are frozen while wages rise, more people end up paying higher rates of tax, even though the rates themselves haven't changed.
    Standing charge
    The fixed daily amount on an energy bill that you pay however much energy you use.
    Gilts
    UK government bonds, which is how the government borrows. If investors get nervous, gilt yields rise and borrowing gets more expensive.
    Red Book
    The full official Budget document, published on GOV.UK once the speech ends.
